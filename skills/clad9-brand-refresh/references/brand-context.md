# Clad9 brand context

Researched from the live site on 2026-09-10. Re-verify with `/clad9-brand-refresh`.

## What Clad9 is

Clad9 (also "C9") is an AI wardrobe app and digital closet at **clad9.com**. Operated by **Hadaa, Inc.** — the same company behind hadaa.app (AI landscape design). Cross-promotion between the two is an established pattern; the Clad9 blog has a "Sister App" category.

One sentence: *Clad9 turns a batch of closet photos into a digital wardrobe, then hands you a finished outfit every morning built from clothes you already own — matched to your body shape, skin tone, the weather and your calendar.*

Also claimed on the homepage: harmony scoring, weather intelligence with a layering "shed strategy", outfit collages saved into lookbooks, visual search, **Style DNA**, week planning, travel packing lists, **cost-per-wear**, wardrobe gap analysis, smart outfit rotation, resale value estimates, a community feed, and a **Clad9 Chrome extension** for capturing items from shopping sites.

Alternative ingestion methods worth using as post angles: photograph it, forward an order confirmation or receipt, drop in an order screenshot, or "dissect" a single outfit photo so each garment is catalogued separately.

## Pricing — the hard rule

**Never state a price. There is no public one.** `/pricing`, `/about` and `/how-it-works` all 404.

What you may say, and nothing beyond it:
- "Free to start" / "Get started free"
- "14-day free trial, no credit card required" (this is the exact promise on `/users/sign_up`)

Terms §7 confirms paid auto-renewing plans exist, but the price is only revealed inside the app. If someone asks the price in a comment, say the trial is free and 14 days with no card, and point to the site — do not guess or imply a figure.

## What you must not claim

- **No user counts, no testimonials, no press.** The site has none. Do not invent "trusted by X users" or "as seen in". The only badges are directory listings (SaaSCity, Smol Launch) and "From the makers of Hadaa".
- **Not "a video walkthrough of your closet."** `/features` still carries this stale line; the homepage and the feature detail page both say **batch photo upload**. Always write batch photos.
- **Do not claim it replaces a human stylist.** `/methodology` explicitly refuses this: *"not personalized advice from a human stylist who's seen your wardrobe... Use it as a foundation, not the final word."*
- **Do not use guilt as a sustainability lever.** The site's own line: *"no guilt, no lectures, no sacrifice to how good you look."*
- The two research stats the site quotes ("people wear roughly 20% of their wardrobe 80% of the time"; "the average garment is worn only a handful of times") are **uncited**. You may use them, but attribute them softly — "studies consistently suggest" — never as Clad9 data.

## Voice — use the blog/methodology register

The site writes in two registers. Social copy uses the **second** one, always.

**Not this** (homepage sales voice): *"the world's most complete AI wardrobe app"*, *"the last wardrobe app you'll ever need"*, *"everything you've ever wanted, finally in one place."*

**This** (blog, FAQ, hubs, methodology): dry, corrective, anti-hype, mechanical. Name the conventional advice, say why it fails, replace it with a concrete mechanism.

Verbatim examples of the target register:
- *"Most of us don't have a clothing problem — we have a visibility problem."*
- *"'Flattering' isn't a vague compliment — it's a specific, mechanical thing: creating visual balance between shoulders, waist, and hips."*
- *"Most capsule wardrobe advice stops at a number — 'own 30 pieces' — and leaves the actual selection up to you. The number was never the point."*
- *"A capsule built on paper is only as accurate as your memory of your own wardrobe — and nobody's memory of forty-plus items is that good."*
- *"Practical, no-hype guides on color, capsule wardrobes, and getting dressed faster."*

**Mechanics of the voice:**
- **Em-dash is the signature punctuation**, used for the correction move: "not X — Y".
- **Second person throughout.** "you", "your closet", "your morning". First-person plural only when describing how Clad9 itself works.
- **The core move is negation-then-substitution.** The site does this everywhere: "Instructions, Not Vibes", "Balance, Not Rules", "Batch Upload, Not One-by-One", "not AI guesses".
- **Owned vocabulary:** decision fatigue, visibility problem, cost-per-wear, gap analysis, undertone, seasonal palette, neutral-anchored, harmony score, capsule, Style DNA, shed strategy, occasion-perfect, "from what you already own".
- **It is polite about competitors:** *"Every one of these apps does something well — that's exactly why they're worth comparing."* Never disparage a named competitor.
- Short declaratives. No exclamation marks. No emoji strings. At most one emoji, and usually none.

## The honesty stance is the differentiator

`/methodology` is Clad9's strongest asset and almost no competitor has an equivalent. It states that every pairing is hand-curated first and AI only writes the explanation:

> *"None of that structure is invented by a language model. If two colors are shown paired on Clad9, that pairing was curated as a real relationship before any AI wrote a single word about it."*

> *"Generated explanations are compared against every other explanation already on the site using an embedding-similarity check. If a new draft reads too close to something we've already published, it's held back and rewritten."*

In a market saturated with "AI slop" accusations, this is the most defensible thing Clad9 can talk about. Give it a post regularly.
