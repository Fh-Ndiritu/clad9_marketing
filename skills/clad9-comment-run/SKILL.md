# Run one Clad9 commenting session

A post to zero followers reaches nobody. A good comment on someone else's post reaches their audience. Until the Pages have a following, this skill matters more than the posting skills.

## Read first

> Paths here are relative to this skill's own folder — the base directory announced when the skill loads. If a path doesn't resolve, locate the plugin directory (the one containing `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`) and read `references/` from there. Never proceed on remembered facts if these files can't be read — say so and stop.

- `../../references/brand-context.md` — voice and claim rules
- `../../references/platform-playbooks.md` — the Comments section, including the hard gates

## Steps

1. **Ask which platform** if the user hasn't said — LinkedIn or Facebook. Default to LinkedIn; the category conversation is livelier there.

2. **Switch to the Clad9 Page identity** before doing anything. On Facebook this means the avatar switcher and a visual check for the C9 monogram; on LinkedIn it means commenting as the Page, not as Francis. Verify, don't assume.

3. **Find candidates.** Search for posts from the **last 3 days** on: capsule wardrobes, personal styling, decision fatigue, personal colour analysis, sustainable fashion, wardrobe decluttering, AI in fashion. On Facebook, look in fashion, minimalism and capsule-wardrobe Groups and Pages.

4. **Apply the hard gates.** Reject the post outright if it is a personal milestone, a bereavement, a job loss, political, a live controversy, someone's photo of their own body, or a direct competitor's launch announcement. Reject anything older than 3 days — nobody will see the comment.

5. **Score what survives** on: is the audience actually Clad9's, is the post getting engagement, and is there something real to add. Take the best 4–5.

6. **Work them one at a time**, not all at once. For each: read the full post and the existing comments, then draft a comment that
   - adds a mechanism, a distinction or a specific the post didn't have,
   - stands alone as useful even if nobody clicks anything,
   - is 2–4 sentences,
   - never opens with "Great post!" or any variant,
   - **never mentions Clad9** — unless someone in the thread has directly asked what tool to use, in which case one plain sentence naming it is fine.

7. **Stage each one** in its comment box, in its own tab, unsubmitted. Leave every tab open.

## Hand over

List what you staged: the post, who wrote it, and the one thing your comment adds. Say plainly if you found fewer than 4 worth commenting on — a thin week is a real finding, and padding it with weak comments on bad posts costs the Page more than silence.

## The judgement that matters

The temptation is to comment in order to be seen. Resist it. A comment that adds nothing is worse than no comment: it marks the Page as a brand fishing for attention, and on a Page with no followers that first impression is most of what people will ever know about Clad9. Verifying that the post is worth engaging with — that it is real, current and not someone's private moment — is your job, not the user's.
