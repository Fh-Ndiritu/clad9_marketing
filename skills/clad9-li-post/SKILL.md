# Stage one LinkedIn post for Clad9

## Read first

> Paths here are relative to this skill's own folder — the base directory announced when the skill loads. If a path doesn't resolve, locate the plugin directory (the one containing `.claude-plugin/plugin.json`) and read `references/` from there. Never proceed on remembered facts if these files can't be read — say so and stop.

- `../../references/brand-context.md` — voice, the ten features, the claim rules
- `../../references/angle-bank.md` — the nine angles and the content map
- `../../references/platform-playbooks.md` — LinkedIn section

## Steps

1. **Open the Page** — `linkedin.com/company/clad9`.

2. **Post as the Page, not as Francis.** LinkedIn defaults to the personal profile. In the composer, confirm the identity selector at the top reads **Clad9** before typing.

3. **Read the last 10 posts** on the Page. Note angles and opening constructions; do not repeat either. Also check what went out on Facebook recently — the same angle should not run on both platforms in the same week.

4. **Pick the angle.** From the content plan if one exists, otherwise from the angle bank.

5. **Read the source page** on clad9.com and write from what it says, not from memory.

6. **Write the copy.**
   - Blog/methodology register. Corrective, mechanical, unhyped.
   - **The first two lines carry the post.** LinkedIn truncates at "…see more" around 200 characters — the correction goes in line one, not line four.
   - 120–200 words.
   - One or two sentences per paragraph, blank line between. That rhythm is native to LinkedIn and happens to suit this voice.
   - At most three hashtags, at the end, varied between runs.
   - No "Thoughts?", no "Agree?", no engagement bait.

7. **Run the claim gate.** Reject the draft if it states a price, invents social proof, describes capture as a video walkthrough, claims to replace a human stylist, or leans on guilt. LinkedIn's audience is the most likely of the two to check a claim, and Clad9's whole differentiator is not overclaiming.

8. **Generate and attach the image** — invoke `clad9-image-run` with a 1:1 brief matched to the angle.

9. **Stage it.** Type the copy into the composer, attach the image. **Stop.** Do not click Post.

10. **Prepare the first comment** with the source link — LinkedIn suppresses outbound links in the post body too. Hand the text to the user to paste after publishing.

## Hand over

One line: the angle, the source page, and that it is staged. Plus the first-comment text.
